K. W. Snyder Writing — Membership Terms

Plain-English membership rules

These terms are intended to keep the private writing library fair, respectful, and sustainable for everyone.

1. Your membership

Your membership is for you. Please do not share your account or allow another person to use your member access.

2. Private writing and copyright

The K. W. Snyder Writing library and its writing are protected by copyright. Members may read the material for their personal use, but may not copy, repost, publish, sell, redistribute, or give away subscriber-only material without permission.

3. Site abuse and harassment

Harassment, threats, fraud, attempts to bypass access controls, deliberate abuse of the website, unauthorized sharing of member content, or other serious misuse of the service are not permitted. Access may be suspended or terminated when an account materially violates these rules.

4. Wrongful payment disputes

You are welcome to report a genuine billing problem, including a duplicate charge or another charge that the system processed incorrectly. A knowingly false or wrongful payment dispute, chargeback, or payment claim made to obtain free access or otherwise abuse the service may result in suspension or termination of the account.

5. Publishing schedule

There is no fixed posting schedule. I will work toward sharing new writing at least once a month, but membership does not guarantee a particular number of posts, chapters, dates, or publication frequency.

6. Changes and access

The membership offering may change over time as the Scriptorium grows. Material changes to these terms or the membership offering will be communicated appropriately. Access may be paused when a subscription payment is legitimately overdue or otherwise fails according to the payment provider's rules.

7. Questions

If something has gone wrong with your account or billing, please contact the Scriptorium rather than attempting to work around the membership system.
