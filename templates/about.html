About

The Snyder Scriptorium

The Snyder Scriptorium is a growing home for books, writing, curiosity, and the stories that bring people together.

What began as a place to share writing is becoming something larger — a literary space where readers can discover books, explore curious subjects, follow the writing process, and eventually find books and goods created through the Scriptorium.

K. W. Snyder

K. W. Snyder is the writer behind the Scriptorium. This space is where the writing, research, reading, and ideas behind the work come together.

The Scriptorium is being built slowly and intentionally, with the goal of creating something that feels less like a conventional website and more like stepping into a quiet library tucked away in the woods.

A Place to Settle In

Whether you arrive for a book recommendation, a curiosity rabbit hole, a writing update, or simply to browse, you're welcome here.
