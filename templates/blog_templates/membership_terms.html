K. W. Snyder Writing — Membership Terms

The short version, in plain English

Last updated: October 14, 2026

These are the terms for your K. W. Snyder Writing membership. They're written to be readable — no legal maze. By subscribing, you agree to them.

What you're getting

For $1.00/month, you get access to the private writing library — stories, chapters, poems, and other works by K.W. Snyder that aren't available anywhere else. Your subscription also directly supports the writing.

There's always at least one free piece available so you can sample the writing before subscribing.

Billing

Payment goes through PayPal — we never see or store your card details. You'll be charged $1.00 each month on your billing date until you cancel. No setup fees, no minimum commitment.

If the price ever changes, we'll tell you first. Any increase only applies going forward, never retroactively.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel whenever you want — no hoops. Cancel directly in your PayPal account, or contact us and we'll do it for you. Access ends when the cancellation processes, but your account stays in case you want to come back.

Refunds

If something goes wrong — you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or couldn't access the library — just contact us and we'll make it right. Refunds go back to your original payment method through PayPal.

Please don't share the writing

Everything in the private library is the copyrighted work of K.W. Snyder. Your membership is for your own reading — please don't copy, screenshot-and-share, republish, or distribute the member-only works. Don't feed them into AI training datasets either.

If we find an account sharing or redistributing member content, we may suspend or close it.

Your account

Your membership is just for you — please don't share your login. You're responsible for keeping your password safe. You need to be 18 or older to subscribe.

Be decent

Don't harass the author or other readers, don't try to break into parts of the site you shouldn't access, and don't use the membership for anything shady. Serious misuse can get your account closed.

No fixed schedule

New writing is added regularly, but there's no guaranteed posting schedule. Membership doesn't promise a specific number of posts per month.

The site is provided as-is. We work hard to keep it running, but we can't guarantee it'll never go down for maintenance or technical issues.

If these terms change

We may update these terms as the Scriptorium grows. If we do, we'll post the new version here with an updated date. Keep using your membership after a change means you accept the new terms. Don't agree? Just cancel — no hard feelings.

The legal bits

To the extent the law allows: the library comes without warranties, and our total liability for any claim won't exceed what you paid in the 12 months before the claim. Pennsylvania law governs these terms. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest still applies.

Contact

Questions about billing, refunds, cancellation, or anything else? Use the Contact page and include your account email so we can find your subscription.
