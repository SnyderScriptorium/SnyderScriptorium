Steven

As he walks down the street he walks with purpose. No validations needed, no friends needed, just a lone wolf with his own agenda, on his own path. He takes this route normally, but he likes to switch it up every now and then. There's an appreciation that can come with living in an urbanized area. The way one can walk around and always find something new. Every new turn taken leads to something new and up coming, or some hole in the wall you never knew about before. Sometimes he even makes a friend for the moment and gains a life moment to remember. It's amazing what happens in the urban life. The random experiences that could occur in the cluster of concrete, windows, and bodies.

Steven grew up here and although he doesn't have any hard knocks life background story to tell, that sounds like most around him, he has his own. His own tale that lives deep within him. It haunts him in the evenings when things go quiet, or when the trigger moment pops up. Primarily he keeps to himself. Not just out of being an introvert, but for protection. It's always safest alone when your biggest endangerment is other humans.

Step by step he walks down the street composed and deep in thought. As many as there is of those who would love to know the story of what happened that made this lone wolf, it would take one of many layers to begin to earn the trust of his deeper layers. So if you ask for this man's story, check that you have one of your own to share.
