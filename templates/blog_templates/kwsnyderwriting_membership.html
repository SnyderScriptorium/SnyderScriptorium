What Membership Includes

Your subscription opens the private K. W. Snyder Writing library, including my shorter literary work and my novels as I make them available.

The library is organized into Writing, Short Stories, Poems, Vignettes, and Novels. Novels have their own book pages and chapter-by-chapter reading experience.

There is no fixed posting schedule. Writing does not always arrive on a predictable timetable, but I will be working toward sharing new writing at least once a month.

This is a place for readers who want to follow my writing beyond the public blog and spend some time in the private room behind the writing desk.
