ScriptSmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What We Collect

When you use ScriptSmith, we collect:

  • Account information: Your email address, which you provide when signing up.
  • Your manuscripts: The documents, stories, and world-building content you create. These are stored to enable syncing across your devices.
  • Usage data: Basic information about how you use the app (such as last sync time) to keep the service running.

How We Use It

  • Your email is used for account authentication and password resets.
  • Your manuscripts are stored so they sync between your devices and survive reinstalls.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or manuscripts with third parties for marketing.

Where Your Data Lives

Your account and manuscripts are stored on Supabase (our database provider). Transactional emails (such as password resets) are sent via Resend. Both providers are contractually required to protect your data.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data by signing into the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual manuscripts in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.
  • Export: You can export your manuscripts from within the app at any time.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Your password is never stored in plain text.

Children

ScriptSmith is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: snyderscriptorium@gmail.com
