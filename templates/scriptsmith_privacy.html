ScriptSmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ScriptSmith is operated by Panella's Hospitality LLC, doing business as Snyder Scriptorium ("we," "us," or "our").

What We Collect

When you use ScriptSmith, we collect:

  • Account information: Your email address, which you provide when signing up.
  • Your manuscripts: The documents you create in the app — including chapters, characters, story notes, and universe/world-building content. These are stored on our servers to enable syncing across your devices.
  • Device-only data: The app stores basic operational information on your device (such as the time of your last successful sync) to keep the service running. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

We do not collect analytics, tracking identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data.

How We Use It

  • Your email address is used solely for account authentication, password resets, and confirmation emails.
  • Your manuscripts are stored solely so they sync between your devices and survive app reinstalls.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or manuscripts with any third party, except for the service providers named below who process data only to operate the service.

Service Providers

Two third-party services process your data:

  • Supabase — provides our database and authentication. Your account and manuscripts are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase processes this data only to provide database services to us.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (account confirmation, password resets). Resend receives only your email address and the content of the specific email being sent.

No other third party has access to your data.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your manuscripts and account data by signing into the app.
  • Deletion of manuscripts: You can permanently delete individual manuscripts in the app. Deletion removes the manuscript from your devices and from our servers.
  • Deletion of account: To delete your entire account and all associated data, email us at the address below. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
  • Export: You can export your manuscripts from within the app at any time, in a portable format.

We Never Delete Your Data

We will never delete your manuscripts, account, or data on our own initiative. The only way your data is deleted is if you delete it yourself or request that we delete it. App updates are designed to preserve all your work.

Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Data stored on our infrastructure provider's servers is encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in readable form — we cannot see your password.

Children

ScriptSmith is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app before the changes take effect. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, or data deletion requests: snyderscriptorium@gmail.com
